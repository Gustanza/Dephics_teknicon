## 3. Three technical departments (the core service triad)

1. **Structural and Bridge Engineering**
2. **Civil Engineering**
3. **Project Management**

### 3.1 Structural and Bridges Services

Covers design and supervision of buildings and special structures under varied site
conditions. Experience with raft foundations, reinforced concrete frames, structural
steelwork, prefabricated warehouse systems and bridge structures.

- Feasibility study, concept, preliminary and detailed structural design
- Structural calculations, general arrangement drawings and reinforcement detailing
- Foundation design, including raft foundations and pile options
- Reinforced concrete, steel, masonry and timber structures
- Industrial, commercial, institutional and residential buildings
- Warehouses, administration blocks, terminals, hospitals and specialised facilities
- Bridge feasibility studies, alignment options, detailed design and construction supervision
- Structural assessments, rehabilitation advice and remedial works proposals
- Communication masts and special structures
- Construction-stage inspections, RFIs, review of shop drawings
- Structural integrity assessment and rehabilitation advice for existing buildings

### 3.2 Civil Engineering and Infrastructure Services

- Site grading, earthworks and pavement design for highways/roads, yards and parking
  areas (rigid pavement works)
- Storm water drainage and flood protection for developments and bridges
- Water supply and irrigation
- Sewer reticulation and sanitation infrastructure
- Water dams and reservoirs including weirs
- Pump stations, pipelines and reservoirs
- **Tailings Storage Facilities (TSF)**
- Hydrology studies, drainage designs and river protection works
- External works for buildings, warehouses, terminals and industrial plants
- Topographical survey and geotechnical investigation coordination
- Civil works supervision and quality control during construction

### 3.3 Project Management and Construction Supervision

- Contract administration and management
- Project planning, monitoring and coordination
- Coordination of multidisciplinary design teams and stakeholders
- Tender documentation, evaluation and reporting
- Project appraisal and implementation support
- Construction supervision, site meetings and progress reporting
- Review of interim payment certificates
- Final inspections, practical completion support and defects monitoring

## 4. Quality assurance (verbatim capability list)
